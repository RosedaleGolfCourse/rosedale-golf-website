---
title: A New Chapter for Rosedale Golf Course
date: 2026-10-09
---
Rosedale Golf Course & Driving Range Inc. is entering an exciting new chapter as our committee and volunteers work together to restore and revitalise the grounds.

We're focused on improving course conditions, maintaining facilities and strengthening our connection with the local community.

Membership and casual green fees are currently free while restoration works continue.

We welcome anyone interested in playing, volunteering or supporting the future of the golf course.

Follow our Facebook page for updates, working bees and community announcements.
